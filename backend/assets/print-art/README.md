# Artwork for the print collection

`gold-base.jpg`, `gold-overlay.png`, `gold-photo.jpg` preserve the supplied floral gold SVG. `azure-base.jpg`, `azure-overlay.png`, `azure-photo.jpg` preserve the supplied horizontal blue floral SVG and frames. Text paths were removed; photo and transparent floral overlays are separate to support photo replacement. Russian lettering uses Great Vibes and Montserrat (OFL licenses in ../print-fonts/). These assets were not generated by AI.

`petals.jpg` and `porthole.jpg` were generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-09-30, then encoded as JPEG at quality 94. Text is rendered separately and remains editable. Images are embedded in SVG previews and PDFs so printing does not depend on external URLs.

`editorial-couple.jpg` is the existing project image from `frontend/public/invite/ivory/assets/couple.jpg`, reused for the newspaper and magazine designs. Users can replace the default photograph in the editor.

## Final prompts

### petals.jpg

Create a photorealistic luxury wedding stationery background, portrait aspect ratio 105:148. Entire canvas is pale warm blush ivory matte paper. Sculptural realistic ivory and dusty rose lisianthus flowers, delicate sage leaves, occasional tiny pearls, soft dimensional shadows. Flowers frame the top left corner and bottom right corner asymmetrically, only the outer 15 percent along sides; keep the large central area completely blank calm uniform ivory for editable invitation typography. Flat lay, premium editorial natural studio lighting. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no border. Save the generated asset for use in a website project.

### porthole.jpg

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset for premium wedding travel invitation. Portrait 3:4 photograph of one complete airplane porthole window frame, straight on, warm ivory rounded thick sculptural plastic rim on a flat warm ivory cabin wall. Through the window a silver aircraft wing stretches toward a peaceful pastel blue sky above fluffy clouds with soft peach sunset horizon. Entire window rim visible, centered, filling most of image with small margins. Realistic tactile details, quiet romantic editorial photography, no people, no text, no letters, no watermark.

## Supplied collection update

The five `*-supplied.jpg` backgrounds preserve user-supplied SVG artwork, with original text outlines removed and editable Russian text rendered separately. Mapping: blue = Blue and White Floral Wedding Invitation (4).svg; clay = Светлое Акварель Бохо Цветы Свадебное Пригласительное.svg; arch = Фиолетовое и Розовое Цветок Геометрический Цветочное Свадебное Приглашение.svg; vow = Beige Watercolor Illustrated Wedding Invitation.svg; noir = White & Grey Floral Watercolor Wedding Save the Date Invitation (1).svg.

Nickainley names use the original Cyrillic-capable Fontfabric font. Vow uses Great Vibes as the user-approved temporary substitute for Anastasia Scr. Noir uses Cormorant Garamond, including the editable initials. Backgrounds are 1240 × 1748 pixels for A6 at 300 dpi.

The olive-supplied.jpg background comes from the user-supplied White Brown Elegant Minimalist Save The Date Invitation.svg, keeping artwork groups 0–4 and replacing outlined text with editable Cormorant Garamond. Supplied portrait backgrounds are rendered from a viewBox of 0.6 0.6 296 417 (noir: 0.6 0.6 296 408 to exclude the source's bottom strip), with preserveAspectRatio=none at 1240 × 1748. Transparency is flattened before JPEG encoding, including gold-base and azure-base, to prevent black edge pixels.
